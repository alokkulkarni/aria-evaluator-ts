# Tier 3 — Digital PR & authority

The slowest but highest-ceiling tier: earned media, expert citations, and an entity record. These build the **authority** that no on-page change can manufacture. Run continuously, alongside Tiers 1–2.

---

## 1. Reactive PR — get quoted (fastest authority links)
Sign up as a source and answer journalist queries on AI safety/evaluation:
- **Connectively** (formerly HARO), **Qwoted**, **Featured** (terkel).
- **Beats to answer:** AI agent reliability, LLM security / prompt injection, AI red teaming, AI compliance (EU AI Act, FCA), "is my chatbot safe," AI in regulated industries.
- **Response rules:** answer within an hour, 2–3 tight paragraphs, one concrete data point, a credentialed attribution ("<Name>, founder of ARIA Evaluator"). No fluff, no link-begging — the outlet links the source.

## 2. Proactive pitches (templates)

**Guest post / contributed article:**
> Subject: Contributed piece — why single-judge AI evals miss production failures
>
> Hi <name>, I lead <role> at ARIA Evaluator (we evaluate conversational AI agents). I'd like to contribute a vendor-neutral piece for <publication>: *"Why your AI agent passed testing and still failed in production"* — covering single- vs multi-judge evaluation, adversarial testing, and a checklist readers can use with any tool. ~1,200 words, original, no product pitch. Outline attached. Useful for your audience?

**Partnership / integration link:**
> Subject: ARIA + <Connect / Copilot / Bedrock> — listing & joint content
>
> Hi <name>, ARIA evaluates conversational agents built on <platform> (we ship a native adapter). Two asks: (1) inclusion in your partner/integrations directory, and (2) a short joint "how to evaluate agents on <platform>" guide we co-publish. Both send our shared customers useful, linkable content.

**Podcast / webinar:**
> Subject: Guest on <podcast> — AI red teaming for conversational agents
>
> Hi <name>, I work on adversarial evaluation of production AI agents. I could walk your audience through the attack types that actually break customer-facing agents (prompt injection, multi-turn jailbreaks, social engineering) and how to test for them. Happy to bring real (anonymised) examples.

## 4. Backlink target list (relevant, reachable)
Pitch contributed posts / get mentioned on:
- **AI/ML publications & newsletters:** TLDR AI, The Batch (DeepLearning.AI), Ben's Bites, Last Week in AI, Import AI.
- **AI security / LLMSec:** OWASP LLM project resources, AI security blogs, MLSecOps community.
- **Practitioner blogs/communities:** dev.to, Hashnode, MLOps Community, contact-centre / CX trade press (for the regulated-CX angle).
- **Aggregators that link out:** "best AI evaluation tools" / "best AI red teaming tools" listicles — pitch the author for inclusion (these already rank for your keywords).

## 5. Original research → press (compounds with Tier 2)
The research asset in Tier 2 is your strongest PR hook. Once published, pitch the headline stat to the newsletters above — "we found X% of agents fail prompt injection" is the kind of line they link to.

---

## Measurement & cadence
- **Cadence:** 3–5 Connectively/Qwoted responses per week; 1 proactive pitch per week; research asset quarterly.
- **Track:** GSC → Links (new referring domains), branded-search volume in GSC (rising = entity recognition working), and any Knowledge Panel appearance.
- **Sequencing:** Tier 1 (entity + listings) → Tier 2 (promote assets, build research) → Tier 3 (PR amplifies the research). They reinforce each other; don't run Tier 3 cold without the assets from Tiers 1–2.
